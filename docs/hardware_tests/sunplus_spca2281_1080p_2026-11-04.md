# Sunplus SPCA2281 / XWF-1080P: sustained 1080p capture

Manual test on November 4, 2026 (America/Los_Angeles). Joshua delivered
1920x1080 RGB images at approximately 30 FPS for a 300-second measurement window.
This result uses an optimized build and the two-buffer capture fix described
below. It does not qualify every build mode, hardware revision, or environment.

- Source base: `261887dfdcfd9645b846ecffe3a3f30cfb3f111b`.
- The test tree additionally changed `CvCamera` from one capture buffer to two
  and added the [1080p preset](../../config/config_preset/example/sunplus_spca2281_1080p.pbtxt).
  Those changes were uncommitted during the run; the base commit alone does not
  contain the tested buffer setting or preset.

- Tested preset SHA-256:
  `27cd16f5d8eb0a130799728e459cdd586ea3eca2ca59473da708c6c818916b3a`.
- Ubuntu 22.04 / ROS 2 Humble / x86_64 in the Joshua Docker environment.
- Build flags: `-c opt --config=u22 --config=x86-base` and
  `--@rules_python//python/config_settings:python_version=3.10`.
- Only the standalone camera publisher and a measurement subscriber ran. No

- The camera's original automatic-exposure settings were used throughout the
  final run. A separate temporary manual-exposure diagnostic did not improve
  the single-buffer rate; its settings were restored before qualification.

## Measurement procedure

The camera advertised 1920x1080 MJPG at 30 FPS and YUYV at 5 FPS. The test used
MJPG and verified the active format, dimensions, and negotiated 30 FPS through
V4L2 while the publisher was running. Negotiation was checked separately from
the measured delivery rate.

Ran `//ros2:camera_publisher` with node ID `1` and the 1080p preset. A reliable
rclpy subscriber with depth 10 read `/sunplus_camera/image_raw` on a loopback-only
ROS domain. After a five-second warmup from the first received message, it
counted messages for exactly 300 seconds using monotonic receipt times.

Every received frame was checked for width 1920, height 1080, encoding `rgb8`,
row stride 5760, and payload length 6220800 bytes. The measurement retained
receipt intervals, publisher timestamps, and counts in consecutive 10-second
windows. A final image was saved locally; camera imagery is not committed.

Before the run, the acceptance criteria were set to mean delivery of at least
29.5 FPS, at least 29 FPS in every full 10-second window, no receipt gap above
100 ms, no invalid frame metadata/payload lengths, and strictly increasing
publisher timestamps. These criteria verify approximately 30 FPS delivery for
this finite run, not sensor-level exposure timing or loss-free acquisition.

an outage during the timed run.

## Diagnosing the initial frame-rate limit

| Short comparison | Frames / duration | Measured rate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Original Joshua development build, one buffer | 153 / 10 seconds | 15.3 FPS |
| Direct OpenCV, automatic exposure, one buffer | 123 / 8.032 seconds | 15.313 FPS |
| Direct OpenCV, temporary manual exposure, one buffer | 120 / 8.000 seconds | 15.000 FPS |
| Direct OpenCV, one buffer, JPEG decoding disabled | 97 / 6.032 seconds | 16.081 FPS |
| Direct OpenCV, two buffers, JPEG decoded | 180 / 6.000 seconds | 29.999 FPS |
| Direct OpenCV, four buffers, JPEG decoded | 181 / 6.030 seconds | 30.014 FPS |
| Joshua development build, two buffers | 232 / 10 seconds | 23.2 FPS |
| Joshua optimized build, two buffers | 301 / 10 seconds | 30.1 FPS |

Two capture buffers let the driver keep the next frame queued while the
previous buffer is dequeued. The comparisons demonstrate that a single buffer
limited this setup, and that the development build remained slower than the
optimized build. The 300-second run above is the sustained qualification.

## Reproduction and scope

Follow the [hardware rules](../../AGENTS.md#hardware-safety--read-this-first) and
confirm the camera's capture index before running. Inside a Joshua Docker
development shell, use the tested two-buffer driver and run:

```sh
bazel run -c opt --config=u22 --config=x86-base \
  --@rules_python//python/config_settings:python_version=3.10 \
  //ros2:camera_publisher -- sunplus_1080p_publisher 1 \
  config/config_preset/example/sunplus_spca2281_1080p.pbtxt
```

Use a reliable subscriber on the image topic to repeat the timed procedure;
the camera's advertised or negotiated rate alone is insufficient. This run
does not qualify Jazzy, other lighting conditions, longer operation, camera
exposure timestamps, or other hardware revisions.
